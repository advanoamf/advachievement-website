FAQ

Everything you wanted to know

Do you ship to my country?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Tell us your address and we'll quote the shipping cost before you order.

How long does it take?

Production takes about a month from your approved order. Delivery time depends on your country, and we'll confirm it with your quote.

How do I pay?

We send a secure PayPal invoice in US dollars. You pay 50% to start production and the balance before shipping. Bank transfer is available for larger orders.

Will I have to pay customs or import duties?

Import duties and taxes depend on your country and are paid by the recipient, unless your quote says otherwise. They aren't included in our prices.

What is the minimum order?

20 pins.

Is the design included?

Yes. The design and one mold are included in the per-pin price.

Can the pin have both Hebrew and English?

Yes. Many of our pins use Hebrew lettering, and we're happy to combine it with English.

Can I get a receipt?

Of course.
